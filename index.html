Our Story

Welcome to Anna Cafe, a traditional South Indian cafe created with love for authentic food and warm hospitality.

Our cafe brings the nostalgic feeling of an old South Indian coffee house. From the aroma of freshly brewed filter coffee to the sound of crispy dosas on the tawa, every visit is a journey into traditional South Indian flavors.

We believe that good food brings people together. Our menu is inspired by classic recipes enjoyed by generations.

Why Anna Cafe?

  • Authentic South Indian recipes
  • Fresh ingredients
  • Traditional filter coffee
  • Warm and friendly atmosphere
  • Vintage South Indian cafe experience
